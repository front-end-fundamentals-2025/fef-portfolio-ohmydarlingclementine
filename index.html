About Me

Hello! My name is Lina, and I'm passionate about creativity, fashion, technology, and making things that connect with people. I'm currently studying New Media Design, and I enjoy learning how to express ideas through both visuals and code.

I believe that design is not just about how things look, but how they make people feel. Whether it's a simple webpage or a small interactive project, I try to create with care, clarity, and a bit of soul.
